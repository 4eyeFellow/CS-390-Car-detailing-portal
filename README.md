**Current Practice**

The current scheduling system for Evansville Mobile Car Detailing is through phone calls, voice mail, text messages, and private social media messages (Facebook and Instagram). The owner must check each source of messages, and communicate back and forth with each client to manually collect all the needed contact information, vehicle info, desired services location and time. This information is then used to manually calculate how long the job should take, which depends on both the services selected and how long the commute is. The appointment is put on a Google Calendar.

**Problem**

Evansville Mobile Car Detailing is a new business with a growing clientele. The company is operated by a single individual, who must perform all scheduling, commute to customer’s homes, and carry out all services. As the number of clients increases, keeping track of the various, scattered forms of communication is becoming increasingly difficult and time consuming. Scheduling requires more than just finding an open time. It must account for different detailing levels and add-ons, and, since the business is mobile, the location of each client and travel time between appointments also needs to be considered. Managing all of this manually takes time away from actual jobs and creates more opportunities for scheduling mistakes.


**Proposed Solution**

The proposed solution is a custom scheduling website, utilizing React Native, that automates the appointment process for Evansville Mobile Car Detailing. The owner should have a dashboard where he can define detailing packages and add-ons, set prices and estimated service times, manage his availability, and view/make changes to his schedule. Clients should see a simple form where they can enter their contact and vehicle information, select a detailing package and any add-ons, and provide their service location. The system should then calculate the total time required for the appointment, including the selected services and travel time between jobs, and only show appointment times that can realistically fit into the schedule. Before booking, clients should be able to review a report of the selected services, total cost, date, time, and terms. Once submitted, the appointment should be added to the owner’s schedule and a confirmation should be sent to the client. Additional features may include appointment reminders, online payments, and no-show fees.
