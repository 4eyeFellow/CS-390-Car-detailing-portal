**Abstract**

Evansville Mobile Car Detailing is a new business, owned by Lily’s brother Carson, with a rapidly growing clientele. Currently, appointments are set up by the owner manually through phone calls, texts, various social media messages, and word of mouth, which requires the owner to manually collect information, calculate the time needed for each job, and put each job on a Google Calendar. However, this process is becoming highly inefficient as his business grows. This project proposes a simple web-based scheduling system designed specifically for the needs of a mobile car detailing business. Clients would be able to provide their information, select detailing services and add-ons, and choose from available appointment times. The system would automatically calculate the estimated time needed for each job, while accounting for the selected services and the travel time. This project also leaves room for expansion to include reminders, payments, and no-shows fees if time allows. The goal is to reduce the amount of manual scheduling required by the owner, while also creating a simple, reliable, and professional scheduling experience for clients.


**Current Practice**

The current scheduling system for Evansville Mobile Car Detailing is through phone calls, voice mail, text messages, and private social media messages (Facebook and Instagram). The owner must check each source of messages, and communicate back and forth with each client to manually collect all the needed contact information, vehicle info, desired services location and time. This information is then used to manually calculate how long the job should take, which depends on both the services selected and how long the commute is. The appointment is manually put on a Google Calendar.

**Problem**

Evansville Mobile Car Detailing is a new business with a growing clientele. The company is operated by a single individual, who must perform all scheduling, commute to customer’s homes, and carry out all services. As the number of clients increases, keeping track of the various, scattered forms of communication is becoming increasingly difficult and time consuming. Scheduling requires more than just finding an open time. It must account for different detailing levels and add-ons, and, since the business is mobile, the location of each client and travel time between appointments also needs to be considered. Managing all of this manually takes time away from actual jobs and creates more opportunities for scheduling mistakes.


**Proposed Solution**

The proposed solution is a subdomain of the client's current website to handle scheduling to automate the appointment process for Evansville Mobile Car Detailing. The owner should have a dashboard where he can define detailing packages and add-ons, set prices and estimated service times, manage his availability, and view/make changes to his schedule. Clients should see a simple form where they can enter their contact and vehicle condition, select a detailing package and any add-ons, and provide their service location. The system should then calculate the total time required for the appointment, including the selected services and travel time between jobs, and only show appointment times that can realistically fit into the schedule. There should also be client and admin accounts that allow for either storing information or managing the schedule and package information respectively. The account should also store future appointments and allow for clients to cancel, given that they are more than a week away from the appointment. Before booking, clients should be able to review a report of the selected services, total cost, date, time, and terms. Once submitted, the appointment should be added to the owner’s schedule and a confirmation should be sent to the client.
Additional features may include appointment reminders, online payments, and no-show fees.
